◈ OUR STORY The Sound
of After Dark

Born in the electric glow of neon boulevards and smoky dance floors, Late Nyt's is a living time capsule — a sanctuary where the synthesizers never stop and the bass lines run deep into the night.

We curate the finest cuts from the golden decades of new wave, synth-pop, R&B, funk, and post-punk — music that defined a generation and refuses to die. Every track here is a story. Every beat, a time machine.

Whether you lived it firsthand or found it on a dusty cassette at a thrift store — this place is yours. Pop in the tape. Crank the dial. The night belongs to us.
